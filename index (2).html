What sets us apart

Built for students who are tired of
searching in the dark.

Every feature exists because something was missing from every other scholarship site in New Zealand.

🗺️

Interactive Map Search

Hover over any region and see what's available right there. No other NZ scholarship website offers this. Find local, regional, and national scholarships visually.

🔔

Smart Deadline Alerts

Get email or SMS reminders at 1 month, 2 weeks, and 3 days before each deadline. Never miss an opportunity because the date crept up on you.

🎯

Real Filtering

Filter by ethnicity, gender, sport, subject, financial need, year level, and more. Categories organised the way students actually think about themselves.

📋

Full, Clear Descriptions

No vague one-liners. Every scholarship has a complete description, eligibility criteria, what the judges look for, and tips from past recipients.

🤝

Tutor Matching

Find a specialist tutor fast. We partner with local tutors in every subject so you can spend more time learning and less time searching.

💼

Hidden Business Scholarships

We actively find scholarships offered by local businesses that aren't listed anywhere else — uncovering money that most students never even knew existed.

Struggling with a subject? Find a tutor.

We partner with local tutors who specialise in specific subjects. Students can find the right help fast — no long searches, no guesswork.
